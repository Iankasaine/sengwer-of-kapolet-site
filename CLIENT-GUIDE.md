# Managing Your Projects

You manage the projects on your website from **Airtable** — a page that works like a simple spreadsheet. You don't need to touch the website itself.

You'll be sent a link and an invitation. Sign in once, bookmark the page, and that's the only place you ever need to go.

---

## Adding a new project

1. Open the **Projects** table.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click the **+** to add a new row.
3. Click into the row and fill in:

   - **Name** — the project title, e.g. *Kapolet Wetlands Restoration*
   - **Description** — one to three sentences explaining the project
   - **Status** — choose **Ongoing** or **Completed**
   - **Images** — click the **+** and either drag photos in or pick them from your device. **The first photo is the one that shows on the card**, so put your best one first. You can drag the photos to reorder them.
   - **Category** *(optional)* — which of your objectives it belongs to
   - **Link** *(optional)* — a web page or article about the project
   - **Date** *(optional)* — when it happened or started
   - **Order** *(optional)* — type `1` for the project you want shown first, `2` for the next, and so on

4. When you're happy with it, **tick the Published box**.

Wait about two minutes, then refresh your website. The project will be there.

> **Nothing appears on the website until you tick Published.** You can start a project, leave it half-finished for a week, and no one will see it. That box is your "go live" switch.

## Editing a project

Click into any cell and change it. Tick, untick, retype — it all works like a spreadsheet. Your change appears on the website a couple of minutes later.

## Removing a project from the website

**Untick the Published box.** The project disappears from the website but stays in your list, so you can bring it back any time by ticking the box again.

Only delete the whole row if you're certain you'll never want it — deleting removes the photos too, and it can't be undone.

## Changing the order projects appear in

Type numbers into the **Order** column: `1` for the project you want first, `2` for second, and so on. Projects without a number appear after the numbered ones, newest first.

---

## A few tips

- **Photos straight from a phone or camera are fine.** They're automatically resized for the web, so you don't need to shrink them first. Big files just take a little longer to upload.
- **Use several photos per project.** The first becomes the main picture; the others appear as small thumbnails that visitors can click to enlarge.
- **Keep descriptions short.** One to three sentences reads best on the cards. Longer stories are better as a **Link** to a full page or article.
- **Ongoing vs Completed** decides which tab the project appears under on the website.

## If something looks wrong

- **The project isn't showing.** Check the **Published** box is ticked and the **Name** isn't empty. Then wait two minutes and refresh.
- **The photo didn't appear.** Make sure it finished uploading — Airtable shows a progress spinner. Give it a moment and it will appear on the next update.
- **You changed something and the site still looks old.** Refresh the page in your browser. Changes take about two minutes to go live.
- **Still stuck?** Get in touch rather than deleting and re-adding things.

## What you can't break

You can't damage the website design from Airtable. The worst that can happen is a project shows wrong text or the wrong photo — and you fix that by editing the row. Take your time and experiment; the Published box means nothing goes public until you say so.
